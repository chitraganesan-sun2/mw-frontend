## Full description  *(4000 chars max)*

```
MelodyWings connects students who need a little extra support with volunteer tutors who want to give their time — for free.

It began as a way to give students with disabilities and special educational needs access to patient, one-to-one teaching. Today learners and volunteers of every kind use it to meet, schedule sessions, and keep learning together.

FOR LEARNERS
• Create a profile and tell us what you want help with — academic subjects or life skills like music, art, communication and confidence.
• Get matched with volunteer tutors who fit your goals, availability and the way you like to learn.
• Book a session on a shared calendar, or start an Instant Session when a volunteer is free right now.
• Message your tutor directly to prepare, reschedule or ask a quick question.
• Browse shared resources and a community feed of tips, encouragement and stories.
• Everything is free. There is no catch and no payment.

FOR VOLUNTEERS
• Choose the subjects and skills you are comfortable teaching and set your own availability.
• See learners whose needs match what you offer, and accept the sessions you want.
• Run sessions over your own video link, mark them complete, and leave feedback.
• Offer Instant Sessions when you have a spare hour.
• Track your impact — sessions taught, learners helped, hours given.

BUILT FOR TRUST AND SAFETY
• Sign in with Google. Your email and phone number are never shown to other users.
• Every account goes through onboarding and verification before sessions can begin.
• Report posts, messages or people; moderators review every report.
• Delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.

WHO IT IS FOR
MelodyWings is designed for learners aged 13 and over (younger learners can take part with a parent or guardian). It is used by students, parents, teachers, and volunteers around the world.

MelodyWings is a not-for-profit initiative. If it helps you, you can support it through the donate page — but the app itself will always be free to use.
```
